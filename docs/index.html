Parametric CAD,
free for everyone.

OpenPara is an open-source modeller for parts and assemblies. Draw a sketch, give it dimensions, and build on it. Change a number later and everything that depends on it is rebuilt.

Download for Windows (0.8.5)See what it does

No account and no subscription. Linux and macOS run it from source.

What is in it

The modeller is the core. The rest are add-ons that install from inside the program, so you only carry what you use.

Part of the programWhat it is forComes as
PartsConstrained sketches, then extrude, cut, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet and chamfer. Named variables and formulas drive the dimensions.Built in
AssembliesParts and sub-assemblies held together by mates. Positions, mate values and pattern spacing can be formulas.Built in
Part GeneratorSpur, helical, herringbone and ring gears, gear trains and planetary sets, from a few numbers.Add-on
Truss GeneratorTurns the lines of a sketch into members cut to length with angled ends, and exports the cut list.Add-on
SimulationFrames and trusses (1D FEA), flat parts under load (2D FEA) and flow around a profile (2D CFD).Add-on
VisualizeLit previews and ray-traced pictures and videos of a part or an assembly.Add-on

Test it before you build it

The Simulation add-on works on a sketch or on a slice of a part, so a question about strength or flow can be answered while the design is still easy to change.

Gears that fit the space you have

Type the centre distance you have room for. OpenPara picks the tooth counts, adds the profile shift needed to reach that distance exactly, and tells you where to put each gear.

  • Trains of up to three gears, straight or bent at the middle gear
  • Planetary sets, with the planet counts that work and the ratios for each way of holding it
  • Each gear opens as its own part, ready to edit
