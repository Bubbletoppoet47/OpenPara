About OpenPara

OpenPara aims to be a parametric CAD program that anyone can use, from someone making a part for a home project to an engineer who wants a modeller they can read and extend.

Get involved

  • Try it and tell us. Report problems and ideas on the issues page.
  • Read or change the code. It is all on GitHub.
  • Share your parts and guides. More generators (keyways, bore shapes and other families) are planned.

Built with

build123d and OpenCascade for geometry, PyQt6 for the interface, pyvista / VTK for 3D views, gmsh for meshing, and Mitsuba 3 for rendering. Thank you to everyone who made those.
