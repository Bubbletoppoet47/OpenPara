About OpenPara

Free software, built in the open.

OpenPara aims to be a parametric CAD program that anyone can use: someone making a part for a home project, a student, or an engineer who wants a modeller they can read and extend.

Licence

OpenPara is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3. You may use it, study it, change it and share it, and if you share a changed version it must stay free in the same way. The full text is in the LICENSE file.

Contact

For questions about the project, working together, or anything that is not a fault report, write to inquiries.openpara@gmail.com.

Get involved

  • Try it and tell us. Report problems and ideas on the issues page.
  • Read or change the code. It is all on GitHub.
  • Share your parts and guides. More generators (keyways, bore shapes and other families) are planned.

Built with

build123d and OpenCascade for geometry, PyQt6 for the interface, and pyvista / VTK for 3D views. Thank you to everyone who made those. The website is set in Sofia Sans, under the SIL Open Font License.
