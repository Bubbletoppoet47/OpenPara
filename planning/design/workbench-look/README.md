## Board

`quiet-header.png`: the Workbench with a block bar in a quiet blue-grey, dark text and a small coloured letter chip. The toolbox groups carry a colour swatch (Analysis Systems purple, Geometry teal, Component Tools green, Design Exploration blue); the chip of a block takes its tool's group colour.

Options 1 (kit hues on the whole bar), 2 (Analysis stays blue) and 4 (colour follows the tool) were the alternatives and are not filed here; they are on the hosted board.

`source/Main.dc.html` is the one board with a `scheme` setting (1 to 4); `source/Opt3.dc.html` sets it to 3. The icons are in `source/_blob/`, referred to as `/_blob/<id>` in the HTML.
